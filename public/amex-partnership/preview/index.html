How co-investment works

A transparent, governed co-investment framework

Every institution we partner with is assessed against the same published criteria: incorporation, ownership, regulatory oversight and participation in Vision Realization Programmes. These criteria set the funding tier, and the tier sets the daily rate and the level of Humantic's contribution. This keeps every partnership fair, consistent and easy to review.

Investment record in Saudi Arabia

Humantic Digital has co-invested in and funded human-capability development programmes, including fully funded British professional certification programmes for qualifying Saudi institutions and beneficiaries.

To date, this investment and programme activity includes:

  • 140 programme days delivered over 7 months across 5 healthcare institutions associated with the Ministry of National Guard Health Affairs and hospitals owned by His Majesty King Salman.
  • More than 540 programme days delivered to numerous Saudi-owned institutions Kingdom-wide, covering Riyadh, Jeddah, Khobar, Dammam, AlUla and more.

Commercial basis

Scope and term certainty

The Full Co-Investment rate applies to the agreed Stage 2 scope from a 50-day minimum, under Humantic's published rate architecture, giving both parties a clear and stable commercial basis for the term and any later stage.

The entry rates of SAR 5,500 (Core, 13–31 days) and SAR 8,000 (Intelligence, 19–31 days) are specific to AmexSA and to this entry stage. They reflect our established Saudi-market investment philosophy under our MISA-licensed mandate and do not alter that architecture.

Questions about the partnership rate

How the rate is set

How does Stage 2 build on Stage 1?

Stage 1 is Humantic's entry investment: we fund more than half of every entry day so AmexSA can see results first-hand. Stage 2 continues at the Semi (32+ days) or Full (50+ days) co-investment level. Full is the highest standing co-investment the programme offers any organisation. See Schedules D & E →

Is there flexibility on the daily rate?

The floor is set by programme governance, and every qualifying institution gets the same floor. That is what keeps the co-investment fair and auditable. Within the floor, we are glad to discuss how the Stage 2 days are structured: phasing, scheduling across business units and the payment schedule.

Why does the Intelligence option cost more?

It adds Humantic's AI diagnostics and analytics layer to every programme day. Humantic also contributes more on the Intelligence route: SAR 10,000 per entry day, against SAR 8,500 on the Core route. This is our edge: Humantic is registered with the Ministry of Commerce for artificial intelligence technologies, application development and systems integration, so the AI layer is built and delivered in-house.

What does the Stage 2 commitment add over Stage 1?

Enough depth to move from a leadership pilot to measurable change across teams. The data gathered during Stage 1 becomes the baseline, so progress can be tracked over the full term through Humantic's proprietary organisation-wide dashboards, assessments and productivity indicators.
