How co-investment works

A governed framework, not a discount

We do not apply arbitrary commercial discounts, and the programme does not imply entitlement to a government subsidy. Each institution is classified on its statutory credentials: incorporation, ownership, regulatory oversight and participation in Vision Realization Programmes. The classification sets the funding tier, and the funding tier sets the daily rate and Humantic's contribution.

Full Co-Investment partnerships run for a minimum of 24 programme days. Every additional day carries the same rate and the same Humantic contribution.

Investment record

Humantic Digital has run a fully funded engagement of 140 programme days over seven months across five healthcare institutions under the Ministry of National Guard Health Affairs and hospitals owned by His Majesty King Salman.

Strategic justification

Why Humantic co-invests at least SAR 96,000 in this partnership

The SAR 4,000 per day contribution across the minimum 24 days, and on every additional day, is an investment decision, not a repricing decision. It rests on four pillars.

1Long-term institutional partnership

American Express Saudi Arabia is a premier financial-sector institution in the Kingdom, backed by 50% equity from The Saudi Investment Bank. The 24-day minimum term moves the relationship from a single entry engagement to a sustained partnership with room for multi-departmental deployment.

Humantic commits a fixed share of every programme day for the full term.

2Building on proven value

The 13-day entry engagement let AESA leadership experience the methodology, evaluate behavioural alignment and generate internal efficacy data. A minimum of 24 days gives enough depth to turn those early findings into measurable organisational capability.

Stage 2 extends a validated approach rather than starting a new pilot.

3Established Saudi institutional investment philosophy

The partnership is consistent with Humantic Digital's practice of investing alongside strategically important Saudi organisations. This includes a fully funded engagement of 140 programme days over seven months across five healthcare institutions under the Ministry of National Guard Health Affairs and hospitals owned by His Majesty King Salman.

AESA's tier reflects an established Saudi-market investment philosophy, not a sales concession.

4Scope and term certainty

The Full Co-Investment rate is tied to the agreed Stage 2 scope, with a 24-day minimum. It sits on Humantic's published rate architecture, so both parties have a clear and stable commercial basis for the term and for any later stage.

Humantic contributes at least SAR 96,000 while the SAR 8,000 / day floor stays intact.

Governance boundaries

Terms that protect programme governance and fund compliance

  • Entity-specificThe Full Co-Investment partnership terms apply exclusively to American Express Saudi Arabia.
  • Minimum commitmentThe terms apply to a minimum of 24 programme days. Additional Stage 2 days carry the same partnership rate and the same SAR 4,000 / day Humantic contribution.
  • Floor-protectedSAR 8,000 / day (Non-AI) and SAR 12,000 / day (AI-Enabled) are the approved floors. No further reduction applies within this tier.
  • Exception closedThe SAR 5,500 / day 13-Day Executive Exception was entry-only and does not extend into this partnership.
  • Non-transferableThe terms do not establish a permanent discount, a new rate card, or a benchmark for other entities or later phases.
  • Scope-dependentThe terms are subject to the agreed scope, deliverables and commercial terms of the Stage 2 programme.

Questions about the partnership rate

How the rate is set

Can the Stage 1 rate continue?

The SAR 5,500 rate was an entry investment. Humantic funded 54.17% of those 13 days so AESA could see results at very low risk. The partnership rate uses the Full Co-Investment tier, the highest standing co-investment the programme offers any organisation.

Is there flexibility on the daily rate?

The floor is set by programme governance, and every qualifying institution gets the same floor. That is what keeps the co-investment fair and auditable. Within the floor, we are glad to discuss how the Stage 2 days are structured: phasing, scheduling across business units and the payment schedule.

Why does the AI-Enabled option cost more?

It adds Humantic's AI diagnostics and analytics layer to every programme day. Humantic's contribution is the same SAR 4,000 per day under both options.

What does the Stage 2 commitment add over Stage 1?

Enough depth to move from a leadership pilot to measurable change across teams. The data gathered in Stage 1 becomes the baseline, so progress can be tracked over the full term.
