How co-investment works

A transparent, governed co-investment framework

Every institution we partner with is assessed against the same published criteria: incorporation, ownership, regulatory oversight and participation in Vision Realization Programmes. These criteria set the funding tier, and the tier sets the daily rate and the level of Humantic's contribution. This keeps every partnership fair, consistent and easy to review.

Full Co-Investment partnerships begin with a minimum of 26 programme days. Each additional day continues at the same rate, with the same Humantic contribution.

Investment record in Saudi Arabia

Humantic Digital has co-invested in and funded human-capability development programmes, including fully funded British professional certification programmes for qualifying Saudi institutions and beneficiaries.

To date, this investment and programme activity includes:

  • 140 programme days delivered over seven months across five healthcare institutions associated with the Ministry of National Guard Health Affairs and hospitals owned by His Majesty King Salman.
  • More than 540 programme days delivered to numerous Saudi-owned institutions Kingdom-wide, covering Riyadh, Jeddah, Khobar, Dammam, AlUla and more.

Strategic justification

Scope and term certainty

The Full Co-Investment rate is tied to the agreed Stage 2 scope, with a 26-day minimum. It sits on Humantic's published rate architecture, so both parties have a clear and stable commercial basis for the term and for any later stage.

Humantic contributes at least SAR 104,000 while the SAR 8,000 / day floor stays intact.

Governance boundaries

Terms that protect programme governance and fund compliance

  • Entity-specificThe Full Co-Investment partnership terms apply exclusively to American Express Saudi Arabia.
  • Minimum commitmentThe terms apply to a minimum of 26 programme days. Additional Stage 2 days carry the same partnership rate and the same SAR 4,000 / day Humantic contribution.
  • Floor-protectedSAR 8,000 / day (Non-AI) and SAR 12,000 / day (AI-Enabled) are the approved floors. No further reduction applies within this tier.
  • Entry stageThe Executive Exception applies to the entry stage only. Stage 2 continues under the Full Co-Investment tier.
  • Non-transferableThe terms do not establish a permanent discount, a new rate card, or a benchmark for other entities or later phases.
  • Scope-dependentThe terms are subject to the agreed scope, deliverables and commercial terms of the Stage 2 programme.

Questions about the partnership rate

How the rate is set

How does Stage 2 build on Stage 1?

Stage 1 is Humantic's entry investment: we fund more than half of every entry day so AmexSA can see results first-hand. Stage 2 moves to the Full Co-Investment tier, the highest standing co-investment the programme offers any organisation.

Is there flexibility on the daily rate?

The floor is set by programme governance, and every qualifying institution gets the same floor. That is what keeps the co-investment fair and auditable. Within the floor, we are glad to discuss how the Stage 2 days are structured: phasing, scheduling across business units and the payment schedule.

Why does the AI-Enabled option cost more?

It adds Humantic's AI diagnostics and analytics layer to every programme day. Humantic's contribution is the same SAR 4,000 per day under both options.

What does the Stage 2 commitment add over Stage 1?

Enough depth to move from a leadership pilot to measurable change across teams. The data gathered during Stage 1 becomes the baseline, so progress can be tracked over the full term through Humantic's proprietary organisation-wide dashboards, assessments and productivity indicators.
