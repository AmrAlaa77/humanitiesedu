Who We Are

17 years in human capital made Humantic Digital what it is today.

Built on a 17-year foundation of international joint ventures across the United Kingdom and the GCC — including our former role as the exclusive MENA educational arm for the British College City Oxford — we transitioned from the college’s humanitarian and development division into our Humantic Digital, a sovereign medical-digital entity dedicated to behavioural assessment and analysis, organisational development grounded in human biology and behavioural science, and British-accredited Executive, People and Culture education.

Led by Dr. S. Abraham — Medical Doctor, entrepreneurial investor, former partner at the British College in Oxford, and Chair & CEO of Humantic Digital, licensed by the Ministry of Investment Saudi Arabia.
