?Why follow-through slipsTap a reason to see a small step you can take.
1Procrastination

A task feels big, unclear or unpleasant, so it waits.

Try: write the first step only, and set a 15-minute start.

2Fear of not being good enough

“I am not the right person” or “it has to be perfect” keeps people from replying or starting.

Try: send a draft or a “here is where I am” update. Done beats perfect.

3Impostor feelings

Success feels like luck, so asking for help or admitting a blocker feels risky.

Try: post a Blocker early. Naming it is a strength, and the team can help.

4Unclear ownership

When no one is clearly responsible, everyone assumes someone else will act.

Try: name one owner and one due date on every request.

5Overload

Too many open items means the important one gets lost. Check My Priorities first.

Try: choose the top three items for today and park the rest.

6No reply, no closure

An unanswered request feels unfinished, and the sender starts to worry.

Try: acknowledge when you receive a request, even if the answer comes later.

7No clear first step

The task is vague, like “sort out the proposal”, so the mind has nowhere to start.

Try: Write the very next physical action, such as “open the file and list three headings”.

8Waiting on someone else

Progress depends on another person, so it feels out of your hands and gets parked.

Try: Send a short, dated nudge, then set a reminder to check again. Waiting For can do this for you.

9Constant interruptions

Messages and meetings break focus, so every task has to restart from zero.

Try: Block one 45-minute focus slot and switch off alerts for it.

10A difficult conversation ahead

A hard message gets postponed because it might cause friction.

Try: Write two sentences: what you need, and by when. Keep the tone calm and specific.

11Low energy or stress

When you are tired or stretched, starting feels harder than the task really is.

Try: Pick the smallest item on low-energy days. Momentum helps, and rest is part of the work.

12Out of sight, out of mind

Items buried in long lists are forgotten until they become overdue.

Try: Keep your top three on My Priorities and review them each morning.

Wellbeing matters here too. If someone is consistently overloaded, raise it with their manager rather than working through it alone.

1. Getting started

Humantic AI - Align keeps every update, request, commitment and decision visible, acknowledged and closed, within teams and across departments.

  • Live workspace (badge LIVE at the top): the first time you open the app, choose your seat on the team. This links your Claude account to that role, so your updates, acknowledgements and your manager's view are all connected to you. Everyone on the team sees changes as they happen.
  • Demo (badge DEMO): sample data that stays in your browser. Use Viewing as at the top of the sidebar to try the app from any seat.
  • Overview & Align AI is your home screen. It shows your alignment score, the AI briefing and the risk radar.
Good to know: each person sees the items that involve them and their reporting chain. This is a shared team workspace, not a private channel, so keep confidential HR or personal matters out of it.

2. Follow-Through

This is where the team communicates. Every item has a type:

  • Update: progress information.
  • Request: asking someone to do something.
  • Commitment: something you promise to deliver.
  • Decision: something that has been agreed.
  • Blocker: something stopping your work.

Send to can be your manager, your direct reports, your whole team (all levels), specific people in any department, one or more departments, or everyone.

  1. Write your message, then choose a type, recipients, an objective and a due date. Requests, commitments and blockers should always have a due date.
  2. Leave Require acknowledgement ticked. Each recipient's avatar shows ✓ once they acknowledge and … until they do.
  3. Use Daily check-in template for a quick "Done / Next / Blockers" update to your manager.

Rules that make sure nothing is missed:

  • Anything not acknowledged within 24 hours is flagged automatically.
  • Items past their due date are marked Overdue.
  • Nudge reminds the people who haven't acknowledged yet.
  • Call for backup sends an overdue or ignored item one level up, to the recipients' manager, with the full thread attached. The item is then marked Urgent with a red banner until it is closed.
  • Mark done closes an item, and Reopen brings it back.

Tabs: Needs my action, Sent to me, Sent by me, My team, Cross-department, At risk and Everything I can see.

3. Align AI

  • Draft co-pilot: as you type, it suggests the type, due date, objective and recipients. Press Apply to accept, or ignore the suggestions.
  • ✦ Communication Coach (live workspace): Claude rewrites your rough draft into a clear message and fills in the type, due date, objective and recipients. Always check it before you post.
  • Risk radar: lists overdue items, items unacknowledged for 24 hours or more, requests without a deadline, work not linked to an objective, members who haven't posted for 3+ days, and heavy workloads. Each risk has a one-click action.
  • Briefing: an instant summary for you and your reporting chain. In the live workspace, ✦ Write briefing with Claude adds a written briefing covering what's slipping, wins and next moves.
Alignment score (0–100): 30% acknowledgements, 30% on-time delivery, 20% work linked to objectives, 20% check-in rhythm (people who posted in the last 3 days).

Note: Claude features use each person's own Claude account and ask for permission the first time. The suggestions while you type and the risk radar use built-in rules and work everywhere.

4. Objectives

Objectives are the shared goals that work should serve. Each card shows its owner, the linked items, progress, overdue items and its last activity. Open work that isn't linked to any objective appears under Alignment gaps. Use + Objective to add one.

5. Manager View

This view is available to anyone with people reporting to them. It shows the whole reporting chain:

  • the alignment score, open and overdue items, items unacknowledged for 24 hours or more, and cross-department threads
  • for each member: their last update, open items, overdue items, acknowledgement rate and status (Aligned / To acknowledge / Silent 3d+)
  • a department summary and a "What's slipping" list

Click a team member to see their items in Follow-Through.

6. Team & Org Chart

This is where you manage people, departments and reporting lines. Click anyone on the chart to edit their name or role, upload a photo, change their department, set who they report to, or remove them. When someone is removed, the people who reported to them move up to that person's manager.

📝 Note: The structure is deliberately generic: Leadership, Operations, Sales and People & Culture, with generic role titles. Anyone using the app can rename departments and roles in Team & Org Chart to match their own organisation. Click a department chip to rename it, use + Department to add one, and click any person to change their role title.

7. Productivity tools

Today, Inbox, Upcoming, Filters & Labels, Projects, Project Board, Team Inbox, Comments and Work Queue / CRM work as before. You can add tasks, track time with the ▶ timer, add subtasks and comments, and log calls with contacts.

8. Data & backup

Live workspace: everything is saved automatically to the team's shared workspace and appears for everyone straight away. Only people the owner has shared the app with, as Contributor or higher, can add or change items. Viewers can only read.

Demo: data is saved in this browser only. Use Settings & Backup → Download backup to keep a copy, Restore backup to load it again, and Reset demo to restore the sample data.

9. My Priorities

Everything that is on you, in one ranked list: requests and blockers sent to you, your own commitments, items escalated to you and your tasks. Overdue and due-soon work comes first, then anything escalated to you or blocking a teammate, then work linked to an objective and work that has waited longest. Requests with no deadline and no objective sink unless they block someone, so the most important request wins, not the loudest. Why is this #1? explains the top item, and arrows show movement since yesterday.

10. Waiting For

Everything other people owe you: requests and blockers you sent that are not closed, and tasks you assigned to others. Each line shows who owes it, how long ago you asked and its status. 🔔 Remind sends a polite reminder, and Received ✓ closes it. With automatic reminders on, Align AI sends one polite reminder when something is overdue or unacknowledged for 24 hours, then at most one every 48 hours.

11. Capture Inbox

Paste meeting notes, an email or a chat message and press ✦ Find actions. Align AI lists the tasks and decisions it finds, with a suggested owner, due date and objective. In the live workspace Claude reads the text; in the demo, built-in rules do. Nothing is sent until you approve it. An approved task goes to its owner as a Request that needs acknowledgement, and an approved decision is posted to your team.

12. Weekly Reset

Your week in one view: what closed, what is stuck (overdue, unacknowledged for 24 hours, or waiting more than 3 days with no reply), what is due in the next 7 days and what needs a decision, plus your first move for Monday. Managers see their whole reporting chain. It is ready every Sunday from 6 PM and can be opened any time. In the live workspace, Claude can write it up for you.

13. Productivity Index

Your load against your weekly capacity, your on-time rate and how quickly you acknowledge. Managers also see team load, with a suggestion when someone is over capacity. The weekly wellbeing check-in is anonymous: answers are stored without names and only shown as a team average once 3 or more people have answered. Managers see workload, never individual wellbeing answers.
