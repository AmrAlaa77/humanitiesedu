Sustaining Collective Strength

1. Individualised performance derailers (1:1)

Performance is sustained by addressing behavioural derailers at the one-on-one level. Through structured sessions that build self-awareness and realign behaviour with strengths, individuals become more effective and the team’s performance grows with them.

Our approach helps leaders unlock the strengths already in their teams instead of fixating on what is lacking. The focus shifts from deficits to growth, so people can acknowledge, embrace and celebrate their unique talents for organisational impact.

2. Sustainability phase: focus groups

Our in-house strengths mapping builds a collective strength culture. Diverse strengths are deliberately combined, so team members understand, appreciate and use one another’s strengths.

Every team member is positioned where their natural talents meet organisational priorities.

3. Sustainability phase: individualised productivity derailers, explored one-to-one

Our in-house productivity metrics and organisational behavioural mapping help address what is holding your team back, through a structured productivity methodology.

Identify the unique productivity challenges of each team member by uncovering the psychological barriers that affect their performance and growth. Behaviours and psychological factors are common challenges to getting things done at work. Assessing productivity through a psychological lens accommodates individual differences, helps increase efficiency, and helps uncover the root causes of chronic distraction, procrastination and misplaced priorities.
