TRACK 1

From Pharmacy Education to Pharma Career Readiness

The programme bridges the gap between academic pharmacy education and the realities of working in the pharmaceutical industry, giving participants clearer career direction, practical readiness and a structured action plan.

1. Pharma Industry Orientation

Understand the industry structure, ecosystem and main stakeholders.

2. Pharma Career Pathways Explorer

Explore career paths across commercial, medical, market access, supply chain and support functions.

3. Career Fit & Self-Discovery

Connect strengths, interests and work preferences to realistic career options.

4. Medical Representative Role Deep Dive

Understand expectations, stakeholder interaction, performance realities and progression.

5. Pharma Business & Professional Basics

Build foundational business, professionalism, compliance and workplace understanding.

6. Career Profile Readiness

Strengthen CV positioning, professional profile, visibility and employer readiness.

7. Interview & Assessment Center Readiness

Prepare for interviews, competency questions, role plays and assessment-center exercises.

8. Career Entry Action Plan

Create a practical 30-day roadmap and personal Pharma Career Entry Portfolio.

LEARNING OUTCOMES

What Participants Will Be Able to Do

Practical career decisions, not theory alone.

✓Explain the basic structure of the pharma industry and identify its main stakeholders.
✓Recognise the difference between pharmacy education and pharma career readiness.
✓Understand major pharma career paths beyond medical sales.
✓Assess personal career fit and build a more targeted professional profile.
✓Approach interviews and assessment centers with a clear preparation strategy.
✓Leave with a Pharma Career Entry Portfolio and a 30-day action roadmap.
